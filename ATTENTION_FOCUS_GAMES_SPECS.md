# Attention & Focus Games Specifications

This document outlines the specifications for the "Attention & Focus Games" category within the CogniBoost application. These games are designed to challenge and enhance skills related to attention, focus, distraction management, and processing speed.

## 1. Distraction Management Games

This sub-category includes games where the player must maintain focus on a primary task while managing or ignoring incoming distractions.

### A. Trouble Brewing

*   **Game Name:** Trouble Brewing
*   **Core Concept:** Players manage multiple coffee orders simultaneously in a busy café setting. The primary task is to fulfill customer orders accurately and efficiently while handling various distractions, such as new urgent orders, customer questions, or equipment malfunctions.
*   **Cognitive Skills Targeted:**
    *   Primary: Sustained Attention, Divided Attention.
    *   Secondary: Working Memory, Task Switching, Planning, Stress Management.
*   **Gameplay Mechanics:**
    *   **Interface:** Visual representation of a coffee shop counter with coffee machines, ingredient stations, and an order queue.
    *   **Orders:** Customer orders appear with specific coffee types and customizations (e.g., "Latte, extra shot, oat milk").
    *   **Task:** Players click/drag ingredients to prepare coffees according to the orders.
    *   **Timers:** Each order might have a timer indicating customer patience.
    *   **Distractions:**
        *   New orders appearing rapidly.
        *   "Special" or "urgent" orders that need prioritization.
        *   Pop-up customer queries or complaints that need a quick response (e.g., clicking a pre-set answer).
        *   Temporary "equipment malfunctions" (e.g., a machine needs a "reset" click).
*   **Customizable Difficulty:**
    *   **Rate of New Orders:** Increasing the frequency of incoming orders.
    *   **Complexity of Orders:** More ingredients or steps per coffee.
    *   **Frequency/Intensity of Distractions:** More frequent or demanding distractions.
    *   **Number of Workstations:** Managing multiple coffee machines or preparation areas.
    *   **Time Limits:** Shorter timers for customer patience.
*   **Success Metrics:**
    *   Number of correct orders fulfilled.
    *   Average time taken per order.
    *   Number of errors made (e.g., incorrect ingredients).
    *   Ability to handle distractions (e.g., penalty for ignoring queries or slow response to malfunctions).
    *   Total earnings or points based on performance.

### B. Assist Ants

*   **Game Name:** Assist Ants
*   **Core Concept:** Players guide multiple streams of ants to their respective colored homes/destinations, preventing collisions by managing their paths or flow. New ants continuously appear, and obstacles might change, requiring constant vigilance.
*   **Cognitive Skills Targeted:**
    *   Primary: Sustained Attention, Divided Attention, Processing Speed.
    *   Secondary: Visual Tracking, Planning, Spatial Awareness.
*   **Gameplay Mechanics:**
    *   **Interface:** Top-down view of an area with multiple ant trails and destinations (e.g., colored anthills).
    *   **Ants:** Ants of different colors appear from entry points and move along predefined or player-drawn paths.
    *   **Task:** Players interact by tapping/swiping to change ant directions at junctions, activate/deactivate path signals, or temporarily halt ants to avoid collisions.
    *   **Obstacles:** Randomly appearing obstacles (e.g., "rocks" blocking a path, "predator" insects that need to be avoided or tapped away).
*   **Customizable Difficulty:**
    *   **Number of Ants:** More ants on screen simultaneously.
    *   **Speed of Ants:** Faster moving ants.
    *   **Complexity of Paths:** More intersections, overlapping routes.
    *   **Frequency of New Ants/Obstacles:** Faster rate of new elements appearing.
    *   **Number of Ant Colors/Destinations:** More types of ants to manage.
*   **Success Metrics:**
    *   Time survived without a collision.
    *   Number of ants safely guided to their destinations.
    *   Points earned for efficient path management or near-miss avoidance.
    *   Level achieved based on increasing difficulty.

## 2. Selective Attention Games

*   **Category Name:** Selective Attention Games
*   **Core Concept:** These games require users to focus on and identify specific target stimuli while actively ignoring irrelevant distractors presented in the same field of view. The key is to filter information efficiently.
*   **Cognitive Skills Targeted:**
    *   Primary: Selective Attention, Focus.
    *   Secondary: Visual Discrimination, Pattern Recognition, Inhibition.
*   **General Gameplay Mechanics:**
    *   A visual field is presented containing a mix of target items and distractor items.
    *   Users must quickly find and indicate the target items (e.g., by tapping, clicking, or dragging them).
    *   Distractors are designed to be similar to targets in some aspects (e.g., shape, color, size) to challenge selective attention.
    *   Feedback is provided on correct selections and errors (selecting a distractor).
*   **Examples of Variations:**
    *   **Find the Symbol/Letter:** Locate a specific symbol or letter within a dense array of other symbols/letters (e.g., find all "F"s in a grid of "E"s and "F"s).
    *   **Unique Shape Identification:** Identify the one shape that is different from others in a set (e.g., one rotated square among many aligned squares).
    *   **Spot the Difference:** Compare two similar images and identify a set number of differences.
    *   **Auditory Selective Attention:** Focus on a target voice or sound amidst background noise or other competing sounds (if audio capabilities are integrated).
    *   **Stroop Test Variant:** Name the color of ink a word is printed in, when the word itself is a conflicting color name (e.g., the word "BLUE" printed in red ink).
*   **Customizable Difficulty:**
    *   **Number of Distractors:** Increasing the density of non-target items.
    *   **Similarity of Distractors to Target:** Making distractors more visually similar to the target(s).
    *   **Time Limits:** Reducing the time allowed to find targets.
    *   **Complexity/Subtlety of Target:** Target might be a small detail or a complex pattern.
    *   **Number of Targets:** Requiring identification of multiple targets simultaneously or in sequence.
*   **Success Metrics:**
    *   Accuracy in identifying targets (percentage of correctly identified targets).
    *   Speed of identification (time taken to find all targets or targets per minute).
    *   Number of false positives (selecting distractors).
    *   Completion rate for tasks with time limits.

## 3. Speed-based Recognition Games

*   **Category Name:** Speed-based Recognition Games
*   **Core Concept:** These games challenge the user's ability to quickly perceive, recognize, and respond to specific visual or auditory stimuli as they appear, often under time pressure. The emphasis is on rapid processing and reaction.
*   **Cognitive Skills Targeted:**
    *   Primary: Processing Speed, Reaction Time.
    *   Secondary: Selective Attention, Visual Acuity, Pattern Recognition.
*   **General Gameplay Mechanics:**
    *   Stimuli (e.g., shapes, colors, numbers, sounds) are presented to the user, often one at a time or in a rapid stream.
    *   The user must perform a specific action (e.g., tap the screen, press a button, swipe in a direction, categorize the item) as soon as a target stimulus is recognized.
    *   Non-target stimuli may also appear, requiring the user to inhibit a response.
*   **Examples of Variations:**
    *   **Target Tap:** Tap a specific shape (e.g., "circle") as soon as it appears among other rapidly flashing shapes.
    *   **Color Change Reaction:** Press a button when a displayed color changes to a pre-defined target color.
    *   **Rapid Categorization:** Quickly sort items (e.g., images of animals vs. objects) into two or more categories by swiping left/right.
    *   **Go/No-Go Tasks:** Respond to "Go" stimuli and withhold response to "No-Go" stimuli.
    *   **Sound Reaction:** Press a button upon hearing a specific target sound.
*   **Customizable Difficulty:**
    *   **Speed of Stimuli Presentation:** Increasing how quickly stimuli appear or change.
    *   **Complexity of Stimuli:** Using more complex shapes, patterns, or sounds that require more processing.
    *   **Number of Possible Targets/Responses:** Increasing the set of target stimuli or the number of response options.
    *   **Duration of Stimulus Visibility:** Shortening how long a stimulus is visible.
    *   **Inter-Stimulus Interval:** Varying the time between stimuli.
*   **Success Metrics:**
    *   Average reaction time to target stimuli.
    *   Accuracy of responses (percentage of correct reactions and correct inhibitions).
    *   Number of correct recognitions within a set time limit.
    *   Consistency of reaction times.
    *   Rate of false alarms (responding to non-targets).

This document will serve as a blueprint for the development of these attention and focus games, and can be further detailed with specific UI/UX designs and interaction flows.
